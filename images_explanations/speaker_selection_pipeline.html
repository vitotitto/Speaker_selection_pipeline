LLM Quality Gates — Detail
Content Screening
Samples first 30 + last 10 transcript segments. LLM classifies: does the named subject produce speech in this recording? Prevents speaker analysis from running on unusable files.
Reject → skip all downstream stages
Speaker Analysis
LLM receives full transcript with anonymous speaker labels + patient metadata (name, diagnosis, interview context). Returns speaker mapping with confidence. Near-deterministic (temperature 0.1).
Low confidence → manual review flag
Acoustic Post-Filter
Signal-level validation after extraction. Rejects segments with music, excessive noise, or acoustic outliers. Runs independently of LLM judgments as a final safety net.
Reject → segment excluded from final output
